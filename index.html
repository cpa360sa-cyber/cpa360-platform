The real problem

Many CPAs don't have a land problem.
They have an institutional problem.

The land is often productive. What holds a CPA back is the institution around it — the systems, records and accountability that turn communal ownership into shared prosperity.

  • Weak governance systems

    Committees operate without minutes, resolutions or a delegation framework.

  • Incomplete beneficiary records

    No verified register; disputes and succession cases unresolved.

  • Poor document control

    Critical records scattered, uncatalogued or lost.

  • Financial control gaps

    Budgets, bookkeeping and audits not consistently maintained.

  • Unclear land allocation

    Allocations and leases undocumented or contested.

  • Underutilised assets

    Infrastructure and equipment recorded nowhere and maintained rarely.

  • Weak project management

    Projects without business cases, owners or progress tracking.

  • Limited commercial readiness

    No market linkages, partnerships or revenue diversification.

  • Funding readiness gaps

    Proposals stall for want of institutional evidence.

  • Poor performance visibility

    No shared view of where the institution actually stands.

The CPA360 platform

One platform. One institutional view.

CPA360 connects every institutional function of a CPA into a single operating environment — so the Committee, the administration office and stakeholders all work from the same record.

  1. Governance

    EXCO, committees, resolutions, meetings, AGM/SGM, conflict of interest.

  2. Beneficiaries

    The Master Beneficiary Register, verification, households, succession.

  3. Administration

    Records management, correspondence, the delegation-of-authority matrix.

  4. Finance

    Budget, transactions, bank reconciliation, annual financial statements.

  5. Procurement

    Requisitions, purchase orders, suppliers, payments, procurement compliance.

  6. HR

    Staff records, payroll references, policies, the organogram.

  7. Land & Assets

    Parcels, allocations, leases, permits, infrastructure, maintenance.

  8. Productivity

    Crops, orchards, timber, livestock, water, inputs, harvest, cost of production.

  9. Projects

    Pipeline, scorecards, business cases, delivery tracking.

  10. Commercialisation

    Markets, partnerships, revenue streams, investment readiness.

  11. Performance

    The CPA360 Institutional Score and monitoring & evaluation.

From assessment to action

Assessment is only the beginning.

CPA360 is not a report that sits on a shelf. Every gap becomes a tracked action with an owner, a deadline and evidence — and the score moves as the work gets done.

  1. Assess
  2. Identify gaps
  3. Create action plan
  4. Assign responsibility
  5. Track deadlines
  6. Upload evidence
  7. Measure improvement
  8. Reassess

Outcomes

What a stronger institution looks like.

  • Stronger governance

    A functioning EXCO with minutes, resolutions and clear delegation.

  • Better records

    A verified beneficiary register and a complete Master File.

  • Financial accountability

    Budgets, reconciliations and audited statements, consistently maintained.

  • Productive land

    Allocations documented, enterprises identified, production recorded.

  • Managed projects

    Business cases, owners, deadlines and evidence on every project.

  • Commercial readiness

    Market linkages, partnerships and diversified revenue in view.

  • Investment readiness

    The institutional evidence a funder or bank needs to say yes.
